**被攻击者反应库**

本文件为长棍型武器战斗中被攻击者的反应描写完整参考资料。包含速度对比描写库和受击反馈描写库。

所有描写基于真实人体力学和物理反馈，禁止修仙/游戏化表现。

# **一、速度对比描写库**

## **反应差类**

**• S01**：the soldier's hand is still reaching for his sword hilt when the halberd arrives

**• S02**：his shield is halfway up, frozen mid-raise, as the weapon smashes through the gap

**• S03**：the soldier blinks — once — and when his eyes open, the warrior is already past him

**• S04**：he opens his mouth to shout a warning but the sound never comes out in time

**• S05**：the soldier's head is still turning to track the warrior, but the warrior has already changed direction twice

## **动作差类**

**• S06**：while the first soldier is still falling, the warrior has already struck the second and is moving toward the third

**• S07**：the warrior completes two full attack cycles before the soldier finishes raising his weapon once

**• S08**：three soldiers swing at empty air — the warrior ducked, sidestepped, and counterattacked before any of them completed their swing

**• S09**：by the time the dust from his footstep settles, he has already covered ten meters and struck twice

**• S10**：the soldier takes one step forward; the warrior has already sprinted past four of his comrades

## **感知差类**

**• S11**：the soldier doesn't even see the weapon — he only feels himself falling sideways

**• S12**：a line of soldiers collapses in sequence, each one falling before realizing what hit him

**• S13**：the warrior passes through the formation so fast that soldiers behind him don't know their front line has already fallen

**• S14**：by the time the commander sees the dust trail, the warrior has already broken through his formation

**• S15**：the soldiers look at each other in confusion — their comrade is on the ground and no one saw the strike

## **群体崩溃类**

**• S16**：three soldiers collapse in sequence, each one falling before the previous one hits the ground

**• S17**：the formation breaks apart like a wave hitting a rock — soldiers scatter in every direction

**• S18**：the remaining soldiers freeze mid-attack, watching their comrades fall one after another

**• S19**：bodies pile up in the warrior's wake, a trail of fallen soldiers marking his path through the formation

**• S20**：the last soldier in the line drops his weapon and runs before the warrior even reaches him

# **二、受击反馈描写库**

## **第一层：即时身体反应**

**• R01**：his face contorts, jaw jolting sideways as the impact ripples through his skull

**• R02**：his eyes bulge wide, pupils shrinking to pinpoints, mouth snapping open in a silent gasp

**• R03**：spittle flies from his lips in a thin arc, catching the light as his head whips backward

**• R04**：his nose crunches sideways, a spray of blood misting the air between him and his attacker

**• R05**：his teeth clack together audibly, biting his own tongue, blood seeping from the corner of his mouth

## **第二层：躯干与四肢反应**

**• R06**：his torso folds backward at the waist, spine arching unnaturally, feet leaving the ground for an instant

**• R07**：his arms fling outward involuntarily, fingers splaying wide as the shock travels through his nervous system

**• R08**：his knees buckle inward, legs giving way like cut ropes, his body dropping straight down

**• R09**：the struck arm goes completely limp, the weapon slipping from nerveless fingers, tumbling end over end through the air

**• R10**：his ribs compress visibly under the impact, chest caving inward, a sharp exhale bursting from his lungs

**• R11**：his shoulder dislocates with an audible pop, the arm hanging at a wrong angle, swaying uselessly

**• R12**：his legs scissor involuntarily, one foot catching the other as his body twists sideways

## **第三层：倒地/飞出姿态**

**• R13**：his body lifts off the ground, spinning horizontally in the air, arms and legs trailing behind the rotation, before slamming back down in a cloud of dust

**• R14**：he staggers backward three steps, each step longer and more desperate, then his heels catch on a body and he pitches forward face-first into the mud

**• R15**：his body slides across the ground on his back, carving two parallel furrows in the dirt with his shoulder blades, his helmet scraping a third line between them

**• R16**：he folds over the weapon shaft like a ragdoll, his midsection bending around the impact point, before being flung sideways off the halberd's arc

**• R17**：his feet leave the ground but his upper body lags behind, creating a momentary horizontal suspension before gravity reclaims him and he drops heavily

**• R18**：he is launched backward in a low arc, his back hitting the ground first, the impact bouncing him up once before he settles, limbs splayed

**• R19**：his body crumples in place like a puppet with cut strings — first the knees, then the waist, then the head, collapsing into a heap

**• R20**：he is knocked sideways into his comrades, dominoing three of them off balance before they all tumble together in a tangle of limbs and weapons

**• R21**：his body rolls across the ground after impact, each rotation kicking up dust, until he hits a tent pole and stops in a curled position

## **第四层：装备与环境反馈**

**• R22**：his wooden shield splits down the center with a sharp crack, splinters fanning outward, the leather straps snapping one by one

**• R23**：his helmet flies off his head, spinning through the air, landing five meters away and rolling to a stop

**• R24**：armor plates crumple and chip at the impact point, small metal fragments pinging off nearby stones

**• R25**：his weapon flies from his grip, tumbling end over end, and embeds itself point-first in the mud ten meters away

**• R26**：his body crashes through a thin canvas tent wall, tearing the fabric in a long rip, disappearing inside in a shower of splintered poles

**• R27**：he lands on a stack of wooden crates, splintering them, debris erupting upward as his weight crashes through

**• R28**：his back scrapes across the ground leaving a visible trail, his armor plowing a shallow furrow in the soft earth

## **心理崩溃反应**

**• R29**：the soldier's face drains of color, his weapon hand trembling visibly, taking a step backward without realizing it

**• R30**：he drops his weapon and raises both hands in surrender, knees buckling, babbling incoherently

**• R31**：the remaining soldiers break formation and scatter, tripping over each other in their panic to escape

**• R32**：he stands frozen, staring at his fallen comrade, his mouth working but no sound coming out, weapon slowly lowering

**• R33**：a soldier turns to run but his legs won't cooperate, stumbling and falling, scrambling on all fours to crawl away

# **三、受击反馈组合模板**

# **四、受击反馈强度分级**

**• 轻击**：仅第一层 + 踉跄未倒

**• 中击**：第一层 + 第二层 + 倒地/后退

**• 重击**：四层全覆盖

**• 连击**：每次递进一层，最后一击达四层
