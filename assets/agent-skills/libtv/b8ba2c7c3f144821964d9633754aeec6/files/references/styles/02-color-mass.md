# 2 · 色块叙事厚涂

默认主图：[R01 伊凡](../../assets/art-library/ivan-dilisnya.webp)。可指定 [R03 伊凡娜](../../assets/art-library/ivana-boritsi.webp)、[R10 查库娜](../../assets/art-library/chakuna.webp)、[R11 弗拉德斯卡](../../assets/art-library/vladeska-drakov.webp)、[R13 哈兹里克](../../assets/art-library/hazlik.webp) 为主图。

## 可见画法

伊凡的红布是连续的大形，肢体、机械与玩偶用少量赭黄亮面和棕黑暗面概括，透视夸张服务叙事。查库娜的剪影、石墙与植物以松动宽笔和刮擦感组织。哈兹里克的琥珀色面和青色椭圆光构成大形节奏。弗拉德斯卡将人物、城堡与圆形强光压为明确的图形式明暗组。伊凡娜用植物轮廓、毒绿色面和少量紫红组织舞台式空间。

共享点是形状与叙事优先、材质概括程度高，不是五图必须同一配色。保留主图对应的轮廓硬度与体积简化程度；不要额外拼进全部机械、猎物、植物或刑具。源图局部颗粒不作为输出要复制的满画噪声。

## Shared prompt core

> Shape-led painterly Gothic narrative illustration. Follow the selected primary reference's broad connected color masses, economical angular light and shadow shapes, expressive directional opaque paint strokes, and deliberately simplified material surfaces. Give faces and hands readable structure with few confident accents; organize cloth, architecture, plants, and secondary objects into spacious quiet shapes. Preserve coherent weight and perspective while allowing the reference's purposeful graphic exaggeration in framing and gesture. Let silhouette, value grouping, and a few focal details carry the story. Use a controlled palette appropriate to the request, natural hand-painted edges, and restrained dry-brush accents; keep broad areas free from repeated fine texture, photographic rendering, or all-over decorative grain.

两种模式沿用这一画法：character保留大面和有性格的轮廓，背景真实透明；narrative保留完整空间与表现性构图。即使主图是高彩毒绿或红金，也不强制新人物必须绿皮或红衣。
