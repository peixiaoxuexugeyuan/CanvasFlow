# 5 · 发光史诗暗奇幻

默认主图：[R08 Ebonbane](../../assets/art-library/ebonbane.webp)；可指定 [R09 安卡霍特普](../../assets/art-library/ankhtepot.webp)、[R07 Cthulhu](../../assets/art-library/cthulhu.webp)。

## 可见画法

Ebonbane由红色剑光、黑红甲片和青色建筑形成清楚的竖向剪影，金属依靠分组亮面和边缘笔触塑造。安卡霍特普的金色骷髅火光是局部强光源，亮金与冷绿暗部对照，装饰集中在关键轮廓。Cthulhu用随躯体方向的弧形笔触、成组鳞纹与发光水线刻画异形巨大尺度。

这组的发光、装甲和鳞片更强，但仍是绘画式色面与有组织笔触，不是高亮PBR游戏截图。每张图保留一个主要发光焦点，减少浮粒与重复鳞片。Cthulhu图中的非人结构属于主体设计，不能自动把其他角色画成触手怪。

## Shared prompt core

> Luminous epic dark-fantasy painting with bold readable silhouettes and sculptural painted color planes. Follow the primary reference's directional strokes, grouped armor or organic forms, selective decisive edge highlights, and one dominant motivated emissive focal source. Shape metal, bone, scales, or fabric through connected painted light and shadow masses, simplifying repeated surface motifs away from the focal subject. Make the emitted light affect nearby forms consistently, retain spacious colored dark regions, and use controlled high-chroma accents against quieter supporting hues. Preserve a hand-painted two-dimensional illustration, clear anatomy appropriate to the requested creature, and readable scale. Keep luminous strands and particles sparse and intentional, without plastic gloss, photographic microdetail, procedural scales, or an all-over neon outline.
